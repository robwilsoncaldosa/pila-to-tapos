Pila to Tapos — Product Requirements

An independent helper for a former freelancer closing her BIR registration. It shows her the whole path before she leaves home, then guides her through both visits to the BIR counter, with a number for each.

Revised during 4-spec (2026-10-22). The official source says she deals with one counter, the Registration Officer counter, across two visits about 3 working days apart. The learner chose to follow the real process. This replaced the earlier demo assumption of three windows in one visit. The affected sections are the Core Journey, Screens and Layout, Being Called, Visit 1 Done, the Staff Counter, the TV Display, States, and Product Decisions.

Revised again in 5-build (2026-10-22). The learner chose pre-written cited answers instead of live AI, and a laptop-only build with no deployment, where the phone scans the QR code with its own camera over Wi-Fi. The affected sections are Asking Questions (Magtanong), Getting a Number, Product Decisions, Deferred, Possible Later Enhancements, and Non-Goals.

What the scope said, and what we know now

The scope was a sketch. Here's how four of its lines turned into concrete behavior.

Scope said
"She sees the full path and ticks off documents."
Now we know

One scrolling "Your plan" screen: a summary, then the path, then the checklist with a "?" on each document. Then a "Kumuha ng number" button that stays locked until everything is ticked.

Scope said
"Answers come only from the official source, with the citation shown."
Now we know

Magtanong is a list of common questions with pre-written answers that quote the official source. When the source doesn't answer (her penalty question), she gets a plain grey card: it says so, shows related cited text, says who can answer, and never shows a guessed amount.

Scope said
"Her phone updates live."
Now we know

Progress is saved on her phone with no login. If her signal drops, she sees an "Offline: huling update" banner. If she was called while the app was closed, reopening shows "Tinawag ka na." A number is good for that day only.

Scope said
"The team edit screen updates the data."
Now we know

A passcode-protected link. No citation, no save. Changes show on her next load with a "Na-update" banner. Her ticks stay unless a document changed, and she keeps her place in line.

Every screen: behavior and how we'll check it

Each section opens to show what the screen does and the checks we'll run by looking at it during the build.

Phone Screen 1: "Ano'ng kailangan mong gawin?"

Behavior

  • One clear choice: "BIR: Close my freelancer registration."
  • One tap opens Your plan. Other offices come later.

Checks

  • Opening the app shows exactly one choice.
  • Tapping it opens Your plan.
Phone Screen 2: "Your plan"

Behavior, top to bottom

  • Summary: number of steps, money to prepare, roughly how long. With no amount entered, it says no official amount is listed.
  • Path: "Visit 1: Submit" → "BIR checks your records (about 3 working days)" → "Visit 2: Pay ₱30 & claim," showing the counter for each visit. If she answered Oo or Hindi ko alam to the unfiled-returns question, the waiting step also shows a 30-day settle warning and a cited "Related" note about the one-time penalty relief program for micro taxpayers (until 31 Dec 2026).
  • Checklist: every document with a tick box and a "?". The "?" opens Magtanong for that document.
  • "Kung naaangkop sa iyo": situation documents, each with one question (for example, "May hindi ka pa na-file na returns?") and Oo / Hindi / Hindi ko alam. Hindi greys it out with "Hindi kailangan sa iyo" and the cited reason. Oo makes it required. Hindi ko alam, or no answer, keeps it required, with a "?" nudge to Magtanong.
  • "Kulang pa: ___" at the top of the checklist: each missing document, where to get it, and its citation.
  • "Huling sinuri sa opisyal na source: [date]."
  • "Kumuha ng number" at the bottom, locked until everything is ticked.

Checks

  • The summary shows steps, amount, and time, each traceable to a citation.
  • With no amount entered, no number is shown and the summary says so.
  • The path lists the three steps in order, with the counter for each visit and "about 3 working days" on the waiting step.
  • With any document unticked, the warning names it, says where to get it, and cites it, and the button is locked.
  • Ticking the last document that applies clears the warning and unlocks the button.
  • Hindi greys a situation document out with its cited reason and stops it locking the button.
  • Oo makes it a required tick box that locks the button.
  • Hindi ko alam, or no answer, keeps the button locked and shows the "?" nudge.
  • Oo or Hindi ko alam to the unfiled-returns question shows the 30-day warning and the relief note; Hindi hides them.
  • The "last checked" date is visible.
Phone Magtanong panel

Behavior

  • A floating button opens a panel over the plan with a short list of common questions in Taglish, including her real one: "May penalty ba ako sa mga hindi ko na-file na returns bago ako makapag-close?"
  • Tapping a question shows its pre-written answer: it quotes the official text, with the citation underneath. The "?" beside a document opens that document's pre-written explanation.
  • Closing returns her to the same place in her plan.
  • No free-text box and no live AI. These are pre-written answers from the official source, not AI, and the video and write-up say so.
ⓘ When the source doesn't answer: "Hindi ito direktang sinasagot ng opisyal na source." It shows the closest cited text, labeled "Related, not a direct answer," says who to ask (with a contact number if the source lists one), and adds "Gabay lang ito, hindi legal o tax advice." Never anything beyond the source, and never a made-up amount.

Checks

  • The panel lists the common questions, including her real penalty question.
  • A question the source answers gets a normal-style reply that quotes the official text, with its citation.
  • Her penalty question shows the grey card with all five parts (including the cited relief-program note) and no invented amount.
  • Every quoted passage appears word for word in the official source excerpt it cites.
  • A document's "?" opens that document's explanation, with its citation.
  • Closing the panel keeps her scroll position.
Phone In line: number, steps, "Ikaw na!", "Balik ka sa", "Tapos na!"

Behavior

  • With everything that applies ticked, tapping the unlocked "Kumuha ng number" shows "I-scan ang QR sa TV gamit ang camera ng phone mo." She scans the TV's QR code with her phone's own camera and gets one number, such as B-014 (the app runs on a laptop over local Wi-Fi, so the phone's own camera does the scanning). Scanning before preparing lands on Screen 1.
  • A one-time "Tap to turn on sound alerts" prompt.
  • Shows the number big, "Step 1 of 3: Submit, [counter]," the path with the current step highlighted, and "All documents ready ✓."
  • Called: full-screen "Ikaw na! [counter]," with a sound.
  • After "Done" on visit 1: moves on by itself to "Visit 1 done ✓. Balik ka sa [date] (3 working days). Dalhin: ₱30." The path highlights step 2, and the return date skips weekends.
  • Visit 2: scanning the QR code again issues a new number with a "Pay & claim" badge. A labeled "Demo: lumipas ang 3 working days" switch jumps to it. (Assumption: the switch sits on the "Visit 1 done" screen, and visit 2 stays locked before the return date unless the switch is on.)
  • End: "Tapos na!" with the total time across both visits. (Assumption: time at the office on visit 1 plus visit 2.)

Checks

  • Everything ticked: scanning the TV's QR code with the phone's camera issues exactly one number.
  • Anything unticked: no number can be issued.
  • "Call next" shows "Ikaw na!" at the same moment the TV speaks.
  • "Done" on visit 1 moves her phone to "Visit 1 done ✓. Balik ka sa [date]" without her touching it.
  • Done on a Thursday gives a return date of the following Tuesday.
  • The demo switch is visibly labeled and unlocks visit 2 right away; scanning again issues a "Pay & claim" number.
  • "Done" on visit 2 shows "Tapos na!" with the total time across both visits.
Counter Staff counter (one counter)

Behavior

  • The counter's name at the top: one counter, the Registration Officer counter. Nothing to pick.
  • The waiting list in order: number, time waiting, a visit badge ("Submit" or "Pay & claim"), and "Documents ready ✓." No names.
  • A big "Call next." Then the current number with "Done."
  • "Done" finishes that visit: visit 1 moves her phone to "Balik ka sa [date]," and visit 2 moves it to "Tapos na!"

Checks

  • The list shows the line in order with number, time waiting, visit badge, and "Documents ready ✓," and no names.
  • "Call next" calls the first number and shows it with "Done."
  • "Done" removes the number from the list and finishes that visit.
TV TV on the wall

Behavior

  • One panel for the Registration Officer counter, showing the number being served.
  • A call flashes the panel and is spoken in Filipino: "Numero B-014, [counter]."
  • A QR code in the corner. Numbers only, never names.

Checks

  • The counter's panel shows its current number.
  • "Call next" flashes the panel and speaks the call.
  • The QR code is visible, and no names appear.
Team Team edit screen (built last)

Behavior

  • A separate link with the team passcode, not shown in the customer app. No full accounts.
  • Edit steps (name, counter, order, and for the waiting step how many working days), documents (name, where to get it, required or depends on her situation), fees, processing time, the citation for every item, and the "last checked" date.
  • No citation, no save.
  • Her plan shows changes on the next load, with a "Na-update ang requirements" banner and highlighted items. Her ticks stay unless a document changed, a new document locks the button, and she keeps her number.
  • If time runs short, the team edits the data file directly instead.

Checks

  • It opens only with the passcode, and no link to it appears in the app.
  • Saving without a citation is refused.
  • Reopening the plan after a change shows the banner and the highlighted item.
  • A new document shows unticked and locks the button, and existing ticks stay.
  • Someone in line keeps her number and step.

What-ifs and states

The cases you decided before anything gets built.

First use

Nothing ticked. "Kulang pa" lists every document, and the button is locked.

Saved on her phone, no login

Her ticks stay across closes and reopens until she finishes or clears them herself.

Reopening while in line

The app checks with the office and lands on her current step, even if staff moved her on while it was closed.

Offline

Her number and step stay visible with "Offline: huling update 10:42." It refreshes by itself when the signal returns.

Called while away

Reopening shows a full-screen "Tinawag ka na sa [counter]."

Between visits

Her phone shows "Visit 1 done ✓. Balik ka sa [date]. Dalhin: ₱30," and the path highlights the waiting step. Her ticks stay.

Next day

A number is good for that day only. Visit 2 always gets a new number, and her ticks stay.

No official amount

The summary says no official amount is listed and never guesses.

Requirements updated

She sees the "Na-update" banner with highlights. A new document locks the button, and she keeps her place in line.

Privacy

Numbers only on the TV and the counter, never names.

Empty waiting list

The staff list says no one is waiting, and "Call next" does nothing.

"I'm not sure" on a situation question

Treated as required: the button stays locked, and a "?" nudges her to ask Magtanong.

Building now vs. deferred

What we're building 9 items
  • Screen 1, with the single BIR transaction
  • Your plan: summary, the three-step path (with the unfiled-returns warning and relief note when they apply), checklist with "?" and "Kulang pa," situation documents, "last checked" date, locked button
  • Magtanong: common questions with pre-written answers from the official source, each with citations, the grey "not in the source" card, and a "?" explanation for each document
  • A number from scanning the TV's QR code with the phone's camera, only when the checklist is complete, plus a new number for visit 2
  • In line: number, step, "Ikaw na!," "Visit 1 done ✓. Balik ka sa [date]," the labeled demo switch, "Tapos na!" with the total time across both visits
  • Saved progress, reopening to the current step, the offline banner, "Tinawag ka na"
  • Staff counter: one counter, list with visit and "Documents ready ✓" badges, "Call next," "Done"
  • TV: one counter panel, flash and spoken call, QR code
  • Team edit screen (built last) and the "Na-update" behavior
Deferred, with reasons 15 items
  • "Malapit ka na!" alert when she's 3 away: unreliable with her phone in her pocket
  • People-ahead count: first extra if time allows
  • Next 3 in line on the TV: first extra if time allows
  • Rough wait time: an estimate the proof doesn't need
  • "Call again": not needed for the demo
  • No-show and "Na-skip ka": not needed for the demo
  • "Save this question" ("Itanong sa Window 1"): nice, not part of the proof
  • Live updates of changes: the next load is enough
  • Full staff or team accounts: a passcode is enough
  • Philippine holidays in the return date: only weekends are skipped for now
  • The real outcome of BIR's records check: BIR contacts her directly; the app only warns her upfront
  • Live AI answers to any typed question: replaced by pre-written cited answers, since the proof is shown on video
  • Deployment and a hosted database: the app runs on the laptop, and a phone joins over the same Wi-Fi
  • An in-app camera scanner: phones block the camera inside a website on a local address, so the phone's own camera scans instead
  • Editing the common questions and answers on the team screen: edited in the plan data file for now
Non-goals
  • Not an official BIR app: no logos or seals.
  • No answers from beyond the official source, no made-up amounts, and no legal or tax advice.
  • No claiming the pre-written answers are AI: honesty in the video and the write-up.
  • No names on shared screens.
  • No connection to BIR's real systems. The proof of concept simulates the office.
  • No other transactions or offices in the proof of concept.
  • No invented cross-office documents.
Possible later enhancements
  • More transactions and offices: civil registry corrections, business tax registration, business and property tax payments, IDs, passports, driver's licenses, clearances, and city hall payments.
  • Designing for the wider user groups: small business owners, seniors, parents, students, and fresh grads.
  • Live AI answers grounded in the official source, with citations: the original Magtanong idea.
  • Deploying it so other people can try it on their own phones.

Open questions and assumptions

Resolved in review

  • Situation documents: one question each. "Hindi" frees her. "Oo," "Hindi ko alam," or no answer keeps the document required. "Documents ready ✓" means every document that applies to her is ticked.
  • Confirmed: scanning before preparing lands on Screen 1 (the phone's own camera does the scanning), "Ikaw na!" plays a sound after a one-time prompt, and an empty staff list says no one is waiting.
  • Decided in 5-build: pre-written cited answers instead of live AI, and no deployment: the app runs on the laptop and the phone scans the TV's QR code with its own camera.

Assumptions confirmed in spec review

Both earlier questions were answered by the 4-spec research (RMC 47-2026, Citizen's Charter 2026 entry 28-A, and RMC 84-2026 for the relief note). The three assumptions from the two-visit change remain:

  • The demo switch sits on her phone's "Visit 1 done" screen.
  • Visit 2 stays locked before the return date unless the demo switch is on.
  • Total time counts time at the office on both visits, from getting each number to its "Done."
